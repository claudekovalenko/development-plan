    Foundation + live journey pages

    $3,000

    • Collective/creator site, Statement of Faith and legal pages
    • Creator URL/slug and public 3-step mobile journey page
    • Response forms with consent and spam protection

    A visitor can open a creator's link, go through the three steps and submit a response saved under that creator.

    $3,000$6,000 to date

    Accepted when

    • Responsive/accessibility QA, deployment and handoff docs
    Accepted when

    The full MVP acceptance statement is met in production.

Legal and compliance

The platform will collect names, emails, phone numbers and people’s spiritual responses, and will send email on the collective’s behalf. That brings in privacy, consent, email and accessibility rules. The developer builds the features that support compliance (consent records with version and time, unsubscribe and suppression, data export and deletion, access controls) but does not give legal advice.

Email follow-up · protections for everyone involved

Follow-up email is a core part of the MVP. It carries less risk than SMS, because it only goes to people who asked to be contacted and the system is designed with unsubscribe and suppression built in. It still needs clear protocols and legal groundwork in place before it goes live, so that Digital Collective, its creators, the developer and the people receiving messages are all protected:

  • Approved templates, with correct sender, subject and contact details in every message
  • Recorded consent and a working unsubscribe that is honored promptly
  • Test sends and a full review before email is turned on for real contacts
  • Written agreement on who approves message content and who is responsible for email practices, with legal review of the consent and email wording

Digital Collective is responsible for its own legal review and decisions: the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, consent wording, Statement of Faith, email practices and any later SMS program. We recommend having a lawyer review these before launch. Legal review is not part of the three payments.
